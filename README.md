## About

I like building things across very different problem spaces. I've taken part in multiple hackathons and worked on projects ranging from OCR and satellite imagery to healthcare software, CRMs, and games. I enjoy getting from a rough idea to a working system, then iterating on the parts that don't hold up.

**Areas I've worked in**
- **OCR & document AI:** handwriting digitisation, text extraction, and turning image-based information into structured output.
- **SAR & satellite imagery:** synthetic aperture radar imagery, ship detection, and maritime monitoring workflows.
- **Healthcare software:** hospital-focused applications and operational workflows.
- **Business software:** CRM systems, dashboards, and automation.
- **Games & interactive projects:** building and experimenting beyond the usual web-app stack.
